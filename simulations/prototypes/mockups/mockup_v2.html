What keeps it uncluttered, and what I'd add

layoutFour areas, not one pile. Equation on top, plot, side panel, playback bar. The side panel is three fold-away sections: Launch (A and B as two columns so you compare at a glance), World & view (g and axes, folded until needed), Readout.
layoutOne readout table with an A column and a B column instead of two blocks of text, plus a last row for range, max height and flight time.
newTime scrubber under the plot. Drag it to any moment, like a video. The jump box stays for exact numbers.
newEquation ✕ marker. A small cross drawn where the equation says the ball should be, under the simulated ball. With no air they sit on top of each other. When you add air resistance later, you'll watch them separate. The readout shows the gap.
newGravity presets (Earth, Moon, Mars, Jupiter) next to the g box, and a Clear markers button so ghost circles don't pile up between runs.
newShow/hide toggles for arrows and on-ball numbers, so a crowded two-path plot can be calmed with one click.
keptSpace pauses and resumes. Every pause leaves a ghost marker with its time. Right arrow steps. Jump-to-time box. Auto-pause is now off by default: press Play and it just runs.

This page is a sketch, not the Python program. The real window is built with tkinter and matplotlib and will look plainer, but the arrangement will be this one.
